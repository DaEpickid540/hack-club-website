arcade

Do your tasks.

Four Among Us-style minigames built by club members. Each one takes under a minute.

Systems Check

A pattern lights up slowly on the left panel. Mirror it on the right without hitting a wrong cell.

Set Your Route

Drag the marker onto the ring, then do it again. Five rounds, and arrow keys work too.

The Morning Of

Close every pop-up against the clock. A new one opens every 4 seconds, and every misclick opens 2 more.

While You Wait

No finish line. Tap, click, or press space to flap and see how far you get. Your best run is saved.
