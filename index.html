// who we are

A club for students who build.

We're a student-run chapter of Hack Club, a global network of high school coding clubs. Beginners and experienced coders alike meet every week to learn, build, and ship projects, and we're the team behind CincyHacks.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions where you learn a skill and leave with something you built.

Hackathons

Build a project from scratch in a weekend, including our own event, CincyHacks.

Guest Speakers

People working in tech share what they do and how they got there.
