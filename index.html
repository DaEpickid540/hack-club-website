who we are

A club for students who build.

We're a student-run chapter of Hack Club, a global network of high school coding clubs. We meet every week, and beginners sit next to experienced coders to learn, build, and ship projects. We're also the team behind CincyHacks, backed by our sponsors InfoTrust and Encore.
